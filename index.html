About Me

I’m Imesha Pramodi, passionate about UI/UX design and frontend development. I’ve explored creative digital design by building real-world projects like a beauty products purchasing app and a cinnamon exporting web app. I also enjoy experimenting with small HTML, CSS, and JavaScript projects, strengthening my programming knowledge.

My goal is to craft digital experiences that are not only visually engaging but also intuitive and accessible for every user. I love turning ideas into clean, responsive interfaces where design meets functionality. With an eye for detail and a drive for innovation, I’m constantly learning new trends, tools, and technologies to bring designs to life in the most seamless way possible.

Beyond coding and design, I find inspiration in exploring visual trends, color psychology, and user behavior to create experiences that truly connect with people. I believe that every pixel has a purpose, and every interaction should feel effortless. Whether it’s designing a user flow, prototyping a concept, or refining the tiniest UI detail, I strive to blend creativity and logic to make every project meaningful and memorable.
